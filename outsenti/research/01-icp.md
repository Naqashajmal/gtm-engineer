# ICP — Outsentia (Healthcare Focus)

## Who Outsentia sells to (healthcare vertical)

### Firmographics
- **Industry:** Healthcare services — medical billing companies, multi-location dental practices, healthcare staffing agencies, revenue cycle management firms, healthcare consulting firms
- **Location:** United States
- **Size:** 100-500 employees (multi-location, needs 5-20 analytical staff)
- **Growth stage:** Scaling or cost-pressured (hiring, expanding, or talking about operational efficiency)

### Why healthcare?
- 41% of Outsentia's open roles are healthcare = their biggest client vertical
- Heavy compliance burden (HIPAA, medical coding, insurance verification)
- US billing specialists cost $50-80k/year. Outsentia does it for 70% less.
- Always hiring (chronic understaffing in US healthcare admin)
- Multi-location practices = repeated pain at every location

### Pain signals (when they NEED Outsentia)
1. Hiring for billing, coding, credentialing, patient coordinator roles
2. Multi-location expansion (new clinics = more admin work)
3. Recent acquisition/merger (combining billing systems)
4. CEO/CFO talking about revenue cycle problems on LinkedIn
5. Job posts mentioning "backlog", "overwhelmed", "hiring urgently"

### Decision makers
- **Primary:** Practice Manager, Office Manager, Revenue Cycle Manager
- **Secondary:** CFO, Finance Director, COO
- **Influencer:** HR Director (for credentialing/recruiting roles)

### Company types to target
1. **Medical billing companies** — they do billing for multiple healthcare providers. Scale = pain.
2. **Multi-location dental practices** — 5-50 locations. Each needs billing staff.
3. **Revenue cycle management (RCM) firms** — they manage billing for hospitals/clinics.
4. **Healthcare staffing agencies** — they place clinical staff, need admin support.
5. **Healthcare consulting firms** — they advise on operations, need back-office support.

---

## Your ICP (one sentence)
US medical billing companies, multi-location dental practices, or revenue cycle management firms with 100-500 employees that are hiring billing/credentialing roles — contacted by Practice Manager, Revenue Cycle Manager, or CFO about reducing administrative staffing costs by 70% without quality loss.

---

## Size range: 100-500 employees (confirmed)

**Why not smaller:**
- Single clinic (1-50 employees): One biller handles it. Doesn't need offshore team.

**Why not bigger:**
- 500+ employees: Already has offshore team or enterprise contracts. Harder to break in.

**Sweet spot (100-500):**
- Multi-location practice or healthcare services firm
- Needs 5-20 billing/credentialing staff
- US hiring is expensive and slow
- Perfect for Outsentia's model

---

## Decision maker (confirmed)

**Best first target: Practice Manager or Revenue Cycle Manager**
- They feel the pain most directly
- They own day-to-day operations
- They're the ones begging for more billing staff

**Second target: CFO / Finance Director**
- They own budget
- They care about cost savings (70% is a big number)
- Good for larger practices

**Avoid:** Clinical staff (doctors, nurses) — they don't own administrative budget.
