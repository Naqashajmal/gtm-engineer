## Day 1 — Understanding the market (2026-10-18)

### What Outsentia sells
Office-based remote staffing from Pakistan + LATAM. Trained talent for analytical back-office roles. 70% below US rates. Deployed in 14 days.

### What we learned from their careers page (17 open roles)

**Breakdown by vertical:**

| Vertical | Roles | What it tells us |
|----------|-------|------------------|
| **Healthcare** | Healthcare Ops Manager, US Healthcare Recruiter, Dental Billing Specialist, Patient Care Coordinator, Credentialing Specialist, Compliance Associate (Healthcare) | **7 roles = biggest vertical. Multiple US healthcare clients.** |
| Market research | Project Execution Analyst, Strategic Insights Associate, Data Scientist | 3 roles = secondary vertical |
| AI/tech | AI Automation Specialist, AI Environment Engineer, AI Workflow Analyst | 3 roles = building internal capability |
| GTM/sales | GTM Engineer, Sales Development Builder | 2 roles = they're scaling sales (this is where you come in) |
| Other | Designer, Builder Program Manager | 2 roles = supporting functions |

**Key insight:** Healthcare is 41% of their open roles. That's where their client revenue is concentrated.

### Why healthcare is the right ICP

1. **Heavy compliance burden** — HIPAA, medical coding, insurance verification. US firms pay $60-80k/year for billing specialists. Outsentia does it for 70% less.

2. **Repetitive analytical work** — medical billing, credentialing, patient coordination, insurance verification. Perfect for trained offshore teams.

3. **Always hiring** — US healthcare is chronically understaffed. They're ALWAYS posting jobs for these roles. Signal never dries up.

4. **Multi-location practices** — dental groups, healthcare networks, clinics with 5-50 locations. Each location needs billing/credentialing staff. Scale = pain.

5. **Cost pressure** — healthcare margins are thin. CFOs are actively looking to reduce operational costs. Offshore staffing is a proven lever.

### What is KYC (in case you need it for the reel)
Know Your Customer. Financial firms verify who their customers are to prevent fraud/money laundering. Boring, legally required, needs humans. Outsentia places KYC analysts.

### What is credentialing (healthcare version)
Verifying that doctors/nurses have valid licenses, certifications, insurance. Painful, paperwork-heavy, perfect for offshore. Outsentia places credentialing specialists.

### What is medical billing
Submitting insurance claims, following up on payments, verifying patient insurance. US billing specialists cost $50-70k/year. Outsentia does it for $15-25k/year. 70% savings is real.

## Company size sweet spot

- **Too small (1-50 employees):** Single clinic. Doesn't need offshore team. One biller handles it.
- **Sweet spot (100-500 employees):** Multi-location practice or healthcare services firm. Needs 5-20 billing/credentialing staff. US hiring is expensive and slow. Perfect for Outsentia.
- **Too big (500+):** Already has offshore team or enterprise contracts. Harder to break in.

## Day 2 — ICP correction + tools (2026-10-19)

### Big mistake we caught (important lesson)

First ICP targeted medical billing/RCM **companies** as clients. Wrong.

**Why wrong:** Medical billing BPOs already outsource to Pakistan. They're competitors or already have offshore teams. Selling to them = selling to other BPOs.

**The fix (corrected ICP):**
Outsentia's real clients = companies that do billing **in-house** and want cheaper **dedicated** staff (not shared BPO seats).

From Outsentia's site: *"Yours, not shared."* They place dedicated employees.

| Correct clients | Why they need Outsentia |
|----------------|------------------------|
| Multi-location medical/dental practices | Do their own billing. US billers expensive. |
| Hospital systems with internal billing | Have in-house teams. Want lower cost. |
| Healthcare companies with internal analytical teams | Compliance, credentialing done in-house. Need cheaper staff. |

**NOT clients:** Medical billing BPOs, RCM firms (competitors or already offshore).

**Lesson for the reel:** "I almost targeted the wrong companies. Here's the difference between a client and a competitor."

### The official GTM engineering process (what lethal GTM engineers do)

```
ICP → TAM → SAM → SOM → Territory Plan → Account List → Signals → Outreach
```

| Stage | What it is | Status |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **ICP** | Who exactly buys (filter definition) | ✅ Done (healthcare, corrected) |
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market — how many companies match ICP (a NUMBER) | ⬅ Next step |
| **SAM** | Serviceable — of those, how many can Outsentia actually reach | Pending |
| **SOM** | Obtainable — how many they can close in 12 months | Pending |
| **Territory Plan** | Divide market into segments for focused outreach | Pending |
| **Account List** | The actual list of target companies (in Clay) | Pending |
| **Signals** | Timing triggers (hiring, merger, expansion) | Pending |
| **Outreach** | What to say (message + sequence) | Pending |

**Simple version:** TAM = the number. SAM = the realistic slice. SOM = what you can actually win. Everything after is execution.
